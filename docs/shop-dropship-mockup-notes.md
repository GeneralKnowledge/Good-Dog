## What we can confirm from public Pero materials

Pero’s **Signature** white-label / own-brand range (Wales) includes treat lines suited to a coaching “session kit”:

| Line | Public description | Good Dog mockup SKU |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meaty Bites | 100% air-dried meat cubes: Chicken & Chia, Duck, Beef, Venison, Lamb | Soft Training Bites · Chicken & Chia · **100 g** |
| Meat sticks | Longer-lasting **chicken** and **wild boar** sticks, 100% meat | Natural Chicken Sticks · **200 g** |
| Rabbit jerky | Listed in Signature treat media | Rabbit Jerky · **150 g** |
| Fish treats | Throw sticks, salmon skins, dried sprats (air-dried) | Deferred for v1 mockup |

Also publicly noted: recyclable packaging options, custom labels (in-house design), counter boxes / FSDUs, **drop shipping**, **no MOQ**. Dry food bags appear in media naming as **2 kg** and **12 kg** — out of scope for the training-treat shop.

Sources: [pero.trade](https://pero.trade/), [Signature brand page](https://pero.trade/our-brands/signature/), [Signature treats relaunch](https://pero.trade/signature-treats-range-relaunched/), [Pet Product Marketing — Signature range](https://www.petproductmarketing.co.uk/new-products/peros-signature-range/).

| Pack | Assumed fill | Assumed pouch (approx.) |

| Soft Training Bites | 100 g | 110 × 160 mm (+ gusset) |

Replace with Pero die-lines and approved net weights once a trade account and samples are in hand.
